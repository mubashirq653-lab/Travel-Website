## Brand & Style
This design system embodies high-tier Pakistani corporate heritage, executive hospitality, and premier property brokerage. It is tailored for high-net-worth individuals, diaspora overseas Pakistanis, corporate executives, and VIP pilgrims seeking uncompromising reliability, bespoke service, and verified transactions.

The visual style merges **Regal Editorial Minimalism** with **Institutional Authority**:
- **Prestigious Hospitality & Property Presence:** Deep royal navy foundations punctuated by fine warm champagne gold accents, balanced by crisp editorial white space.
- **Architectural Symmetry & Discretion:** Subtle 8-point geometric star micro-patterns and linear gold separations provide cultural reverence for pilgrimage services without visual clutter.
- **Transactional Trust:** Prominent verification seals, strict chauffeur-dispatch labels for vehicle logistics, and direct WhatsApp VIP action points styled to maintain an executive presence rather than transactional noise.

## Colors
The palette balances deep sovereign tones with precious metal and verified status markers:

- **Primary Navy (`#0B192C`):** Denotes supreme institutional authority and executive security. Used for primary typography, dominant navbars, hero backgrounds, and authoritative button states. An alternate elevated navy (`#0F2537`) is deployed for card surfaces and layered backgrounds in dark containers.
- **Secondary Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`, Accent `#D4AF37`):** Represents prestige, premium tiering, and curated estates. Used for borders, key badges, interactive accents, pricing numbers, and luxury typography markers.
- **Tertiary Regal Emerald (`#0F5132`, Accent `#198754`):** Represents verified status, spiritual sanctity for Umrah/Hajj packages, and quick direct WhatsApp communication channels.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FDFDFD` (Pure clean ivory white).
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F4F6F8` (Warm soft slate tint for specification tables and backdrops).
  - `Border Subdued`: `#E2E7EC` (Crisp separation for light mode).
  - `Border Gold Accent`: `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)` (Delicate metallic hairline outlines).
  - `Text Primary`: `#0B192C`.
  - `Text Muted`: `#5A6675`.

## Typography
The system employs an authoritative dual-type structure:
- **Playfair Display (Serif):** Reserved for institutional titles, division mastheads, luxury tour banners, estate development names, and verified quotes. It establishes enduring permanence and prestige.
- **Manrope (Geometric Sans):** Drives clear legibility across complex vehicle technical specs, flight schedules, property square footages, rates, and international dial codes (+92). All numerals in financial cards and fleet specs should use proportional figures with `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`.
- **Editorial Sub-Headings & Badges:** Always set with uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) using Manrope SemiBold to anchor high-value cards with quiet confidence.

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column responsive layout anchors all visual balance:

- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, max-width container of 1280px centered with 64px outer padding. Division cards follow 4-column (triad) or 6-column (duo) arrangements.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px outer page margin. Complex matrices (fleet specs, estate comparisons) condense into 2-column or horizontally swipeable displays.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px outer margin. Floating contact rails lock strictly to viewport edges with prioritized +92 WhatsApp access.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built on an 8px architectural grid. Division sections must use generous vertical breathing room (`space-3xl` or `space-4xl`) to maintain high-end estate and VIP travel presence without visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is rendered through architectural restraint rather than deep shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Backgrounds (`#FDFDFD` or deep `#0B192C`) utilize hair-thin borders (`1px solid #E2E7EC` or `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`).
- **Level 1 (Property & Fleet Cards):** Surface `#FFFFFF` elevated with an ultra-subtle ambient drop: `0px 4px 20px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`, bounded by a dual-tone border (standard gray with a 1px top-highlight in antique gold).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, WhatsApp Flyouts & Sticky Nav):** Surface `#FFFFFF` with `0px 12px 32px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12)` and a crisp gold hairline perimeter (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`).
- **Dark Tier Containers:** When featuring executive sedans, royal SUV fleets, or bespoke estate penthouses, containers invert to `#0B192C` with subtle radial lighting: `radial-gradient(ellipse at top, #0F2537 0%, #0B192C 100%)`.

## Shapes
A conservative, tailored geometry is enforced:
- Elements use **Soft Cornering** (radius level 1): Standard inputs, primary action buttons, property showcase cards, and status banners adopt `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.5rem` (8px). 
- Extreme pill shapes are strictly restricted to verified micro-badges and chauffeur confirmation tags.
- Structural elements and section cards avoid circular or excessive bubbling shapes to reflect corporate dignity and architectural precision.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & VIP CTAs
- **Primary Executive Button:** Navy background (`#0B192C`), pure white text, 1px border of `#C5A059`. Height: 48px, horizontal padding: 24px. Hover state: background turns `#0F2537` with elevated gold border glow.
- **WhatsApp Concierge Button:** Emerald green (`#0F5132` or `#128C7E`), white text, crisp WhatsApp icon prefix, and micro-text displaying Pakistan country code formatting (`+92 3XX XXXXXXX`). Hover state: `#157347`.
- **Secondary Outlined Button:** Transparent background, text `#0B192C`, border `1.5px solid #0B192C`. Hover: fills with `#F4F6F8`.

### 2. Verified Chauffeur-Included Badges (Fleet Rental)
- Distinct badge applied to all luxury vehicle units (Land Cruisers, Prado, Mercedes, Audi): Navy pill container, gold border, white text reading `Strictly Chauffeur-Driven / Verified Professional Driver`. This must be visually distinct from self-drive services to maintain safety and luxury standards.

### 3. Estate & Tour Package Cards
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px border `#E2E7EC`.
- Top image container in 16:10 ratio with division category tags (e.g., `DHA Phase 6 • Commercial` or `Executive Umrah • 5-Star VIP`).
- Lower content area with Playfair Display heading, Manrope architectural property specs or travel days breakdown, and a clean separator line before the final gold-accented price block.

### 4. Umrah & Hajj Spiritual Accent Elements
- Light gold watermark divider lines inspired by eight-point Islamic star geometric geometry.
- Green star-and-crescent or minaret badge in tertiary emerald (`#0F5132`) alongside luxury certification numbers (Govt License / IATA endorsements).

### 5. Input Fields & Inquiry Forms
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D1D5DB`, radius `4px`, height `48px`.
- Active focus state: `1.5px solid #C5A059` with zero bright-blue glow, maintaining corporate refinement.
- Mobile phone fields pre-populate with the green Pakistan flag and `+92` country code prefix.
